About ScrapNet

What is ScrapNet?

ScrapNet is a smart web-based platform designed to simplify and digitize the vehicle scrapping process. It connects vehicle owners, authorized scrap agencies, and RTO officials in one system to ensure a transparent and legally compliant scrapping workflow.

Purpose of the System

Vehicle Submissions

Allow vehicle owners to submit vehicles for scrapping easily.

Agency Inspections

Allow agencies to inspect vehicles and estimate scrap value digitially.

RTO Verification

Allow RTO officers to verify and approve requests securely.

Digital Certificates

Generate digital scrap certificates instantly after approval.

Key Objectives

  • Environmental Protection

    Reduce environmental pollution from old and inefficient vehicles.

  • Road Safety

    Improve overall road safety by removing unfit vehicles.

  • Resource Recycling

    Promote recycling of materials such as steel and aluminium.

  • Legal Compliance

    Ensure legal deregistration of vehicles from government records.

System Benefits

Digital vehicle submission
Transparent status tracking
Secure record management
Digital certificate generation
