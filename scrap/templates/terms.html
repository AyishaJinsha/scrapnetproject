Vehicle Scrapping Regulations

Vehicles older than recommended operational age should be scrapped through authorized facilities to ensure safe disposal and legal deregistration.

Eligible Vehicles

Private Vehicles

Older than 20 years

Commercial Vehicles

Older than 15 years (if failed fitness test)

Owner Responsibilities

Users must provide accurate vehicle details and necessary documents including:

  • Registration Certificate (RC)
  • Identity Proof (Aadhaar / PAN)
  • No Objection Certificate (NOC) if loan exists
  • Declaration confirming no legal issues

Scrapping Process

1
Submission

User submits vehicle details via ScrapNet dashboard.

2
Agency Review

Authorized agency reviews the request and inspects the vehicle.

3
RTO Forwarding

Agency forwards the request to RTO for official cancellation.

4
Approval & Scrapping

RTO approves the request, vehicle is scrapped, and certificate issued.

Financial Terms

Scrap value depends on vehicle condition and metal weight and may range between ₹15–₹20 per kg or 4–6% of the vehicle’s original price.

Insurance

Vehicle owners must inform their insurance provider to cancel the policy after vehicle deregistration is complete.

By using ScrapNet, you agree to comply with all regional transport authority (RTO) guidelines and environmental regulations.
